Hot Games:

Snake
Guide an ever-growing snake around the screen, eating food while avoiding collisions with walls and your own tail. Snake is considered a timeless classic because its simple controls hide increasingly challenging gameplay that rewards quick thinking and precision.
Tetris
Arrange falling blocks to complete horizontal lines before the screen fills up. Tetris is widely regarded as one of the greatest games ever made thanks to its perfect balance of strategy, fast decision-making, and endlessly replayable gameplay.
Block Dude
Solve puzzles by carrying and stacking blocks to reach the exit. Block Dude has earned a loyal following for its clever level design, introducing simple mechanics that gradually evolve into surprisingly challenging brain teasers.
